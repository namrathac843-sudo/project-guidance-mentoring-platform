What the platform does

📁

Project submission

Upload PDF, Word, PowerPoint, or spreadsheet files. Track review status from Draft to Accepted.

✅

Lecturer review

Accept, reject, or request changes with clear written feedback. Every action is logged with a timestamp.

🎓

Mentor discovery

Find lecturers and alumni by expertise, department, or company. Send a mentoring request in two clicks.

📅

Mentoring sessions

Schedule sessions with Google Meet or Zoom links. Participants get notified when anything changes.

💬

Messaging & notifications

Discuss project details inside the platform. Every action that affects you generates a notification.

📊

Progress analytics

Interactive charts showing project status, per-project progress, user distribution, and submissions over time.

Four roles, one workspace

Students

  • Submit projects and track approval
  • Find mentors and request sessions
  • Read feedback and resubmit revisions

Lecturers

  • Review and approve submissions
  • Provide structured feedback
  • Schedule mentoring sessions

Alumni

  • Offer industry mentoring
  • Share Google Meet / Zoom links
  • Respond to mentorship requests

Administrators

  • Manage user accounts
  • Monitor all projects
  • View platform statistics and activity
